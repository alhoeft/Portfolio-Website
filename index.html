Addie Hoeft

Computer Science Student ★ Aspiring Developer

I am Addie Hoeft, a sophomore pursuing a degree at CU Boulder with a computer science major (BS) and buisness minor. I am originally from New Jersey, but am open to work anywhere. This semester, I am tutoring Discrete Structures and Introduction to Computer Science. I also coach young goalkeepers for Colorado Bears Field Hockey. I love to break down concepts and explain things I am passionate about, so these two jobs are great fun for me. Furthermore, they force me to dig deeper into interesting concepts, thus help me to learn too. As a student, I find myself most interested in back-end and database development. Recently, I worked on a full-stack group project in my Software Development class where I developed the database for a workout tracking website. I am pursuing databases further as I take Design and Analysis of Database Systems in the spring. I am also a member of Phi Sigma Rho, a sorority for women in STEM. I have served as New Member Education, Director of Philanthropy, and Seargent of Arms (rule enforcer). I also won my sorority's Philanthropy award for my work raising money for the Leukemia and Lymphoma Society. Staying active is also important to me as I am a member of CU Club Field Hockey. We travel as a team and raise money through ticket scanning for the university. I love working as team, and am especially proud of my leadership abilities.
