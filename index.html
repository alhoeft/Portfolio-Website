Phi Sigma Rho

I have been a member of Phi Sigma Rho since my first semester of college. It is a social sorority for women in STEM and has allowed me to meet other highly motivated individuals who are now my best friends. My second semester, I was elected Director of Philanthropy. I hosted restaurant fundraisers and a bake sale to raise money for the Leukemia and Lymphoma Society (now Blood Cancer United). I earned my sorority's philanthropy award for that school year. Originally, I joined the sorority with the intent of doing something good for the community, so it meant a lot to me to be recognized with that award. That semester, I also served as Sergeant at Arms - our rule enforcer. It was my job to ensure decorum at chapter meetings and to sit on the standards board. This role allowed me to exercise responsibility and discipline. The next semester, I served as New Member Educator where I taught a weekly class to initiate 30 new members into the sorority. I love Phi Sigma Rho, so I truly enjoyed getting to introduce the candidates into a community that I am so passionate about. In this role, I also served as a liaison between the executive board and the candidates. Additionally, I organized the candidates' first philanthropy event, sewing blankets for a children's home. I have now taken a step back from leadership in Phi Sigma Rho to focus my efforts on other opportunities, but will forever be thankful for the opportunities my sorority has given me. I remain an active member where I enjoy going to sisterhood, chapter, ritual, and philanthropy events.
